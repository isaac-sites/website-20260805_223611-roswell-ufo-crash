## Introduction

The identification of **Project Mogul Flight 4** as the source of [the Roswell debris]({{ 'the-roswell-debris/' | relative_url }}) is the central factual claim of the US Air Force's 1994 *[Roswell Report]({{ 'the-roswell-report/' | relative_url }})*. It is also one of the most debated parts of the official explanation. While Flight 4 is widely regarded as the best available candidate among known Mogul launches, the evidence is ultimately **circumstantial rather than conclusive**. No surviving label, serial number, recovery log or contemporary military report directly links Flight 4 to the Foster Ranch where W. W. "Mac" Brazel discovered the debris. Instead, the identification rests on a combination of surviving Project Mogul records, estimated flight paths, the timing of the discovery and the reported appearance of the recovered material.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsa.gov">[NSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report af roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORT OF AIR FORCE RESEARCH REGARDING THEMarch 24, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2018</span></span></span>



<figure class="article-media-figure article-media-figure-illustration"><img src="{{ "/assets/images/Roswell_UFO_crash_f2c9c2_roswell_report_90b4db_flight_4_roswell_ide_135d57-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Flight 4 illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Roswell_UFO_crash_f2c9c2_roswell_report_90b4db_flight_4_roswell_ide_135d57-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Roswell_UFO_crash_f2c9c2_roswell_report_90b4db_flight_4_roswell_ide_135d57-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low"><figcaption class="article-media-caption"><span class="article-media-caption-label">Explanatory illustration 1</span></figcaption></figure>

Understanding the strength of the Flight 4 identification therefore requires separating two different questions: whether Project Mogul provides a plausible explanation for the Roswell debris, and whether the surviving historical record specifically proves that **Flight 4** was the object recovered. The available [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) supports the first proposition more strongly than the second.

## What surviving Mogul records say about the launch

The Air Force investigation reconstructed early June 1947 Mogul operations using surviving technical documents, launch records and the diary of project scientist Albert P. Crary. Investigators concluded that an unrecovered Mogul [balloon train]({{ 'balloon-train/' | relative_url }}) launched from Alamogordo in early June was the most likely source of the debris later found near Roswell. Charles [Moore]({{ 'moore/' | relative_url }}), one of the original Project Mogul engineers, argued that Flight 4 best matched both the timing and the reported debris.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsa.gov">[NSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report af roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORT OF AIR FORCE RESEARCH REGARDING THEMarch 24, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2018</span></span></span>

The case relied on several observations:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Brazel stated that he found the debris during June, consistent with an early June balloon launch rather than one in July.
* Flight 4 was regarded by Air Force investigators as one of the few early Mogul flights that was not recovered by its launch team.
* The reported debris—foil-like material, wooden sticks, rubber fragments and paper-like components—resembled the mixture of radar reflector materials, balloons and other equipment used in Project Mogul balloon trains.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://muller.lbl.gov/teaching/physics10/Roswell/USMogulReport.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: muller.lbl.gov">[Muller Lab]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">muller.lbl.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Muller Lab Project Mogul</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Muller Lab Project Mogul</span></span></span>

</div>

However, the documentary record is not as straightforward as the Air Force summary sometimes suggests. Crary's diary entry for 4 June records poor weather and notes, "No balloon flights again on account of clouds," before describing a smaller sonobuoy balloon test. Critics argue that this wording indicates that the planned full Mogul launch did not occur exactly as later described. Defenders of the Air Force interpretation respond that balloon activity nevertheless took place that day and that terminology in the field records was not always consistent with later flight numbering.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsa.gov">[NSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report af roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORT OF AIR FORCE RESEARCH REGARDING THEMarch 24, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2018</span></span></span>

As a result, the historical debate has shifted from whether a balloon operation occurred to **what configuration actually flew**, and whether it corresponded to what later became known as Flight 4.

## Why the projected route points towards the ranch

The geographical argument is one of the stronger elements supporting the Flight 4 hypothesis.

Project Mogul balloons launched from Alamogordo were intended to drift with prevailing winds while carrying long trains of balloons, radar reflectors and experimental acoustic equipment. Meteorological reconstructions and launch calculations used during the Air Force investigation suggested that an unrecovered balloon train released in early June could have travelled north-east towards the general area where Brazel's ranch lay.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsa.gov">[NSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report af roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORT OF AIR FORCE RESEARCH REGARDING THEMarch 24, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2018</span></span></span>

Importantly, this is a **probabilistic** rather than documentary connection.

No surviving tracking record follows a specific balloon continuously from launch to the Foster Ranch. Instead, investigators combined:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* recorded launch dates,
* prevailing wind information,
* expected balloon drift,
* the absence of recovery records, and
* the reported discovery date.

</div>

Taken together, these factors make Flight 4 geographically plausible. They do not establish a continuous chain of evidence linking one identifiable balloon train to one identifiable debris field.

This distinction matters because drift modelling can narrow candidates but cannot substitute for direct recovery documentation.

## What evidence is missing from the identification

The Flight 4 explanation remains incomplete because several pieces of evidence that would make the identification definitive no longer exist or have never been found.

Most importantly, there is:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* no surviving military recovery report identifying the ranch debris as Flight 4;
* no recovered equipment bearing serial numbers that match Mogul inventory records;
* no contemporary document stating that Flight 4 landed on Brazel's property;
* no surviving chain-of-custody records tracing recovered components from the ranch back to Project Mogul personnel.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeGovernment Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico &#124;...</span></span></span>

</div>

The Government Accountability Office's investigation also found that some [Roswell Army Air]({{ 'roswell-army-air/' | relative_url }}) Field administrative records had been destroyed, making it impossible to determine whether additional documentation once existed. Although the GAO found no evidence of hidden records proving an extraterrestrial recovery, it likewise could not recover records that might have documented routine handling of the debris.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeGovernment Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico &#124;...</span></span></span>

Consequently, the historical record contains an unavoidable evidential gap between the balloon programme and the debris recovered from the ranch.

## Why historians continue to disagree

The disagreement over Flight 4 is less about the existence of Project Mogul than about historical standards of proof.

Researchers who accept the Air Force conclusion generally argue that independent lines of evidence converge:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Project Mogul equipment matches many descriptions of the debris.(#endnote-3 "Endnote 3")<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://muller.lbl.gov/teaching/physics10/Roswell/USMogulReport.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: muller.lbl.gov">[muller.lbl.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">muller.lbl.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Muller Lab Project Mogul</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Muller Lab Project Mogul</span></span></span>
* An unrecovered early June balloon operation existed.
* The estimated drift path reaches the appropriate region.
* No competing explanation is supported by stronger contemporary documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsa.gov">[NSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report af roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORT OF AIR FORCE RESEARCH REGARDING THEMarch 24, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2018</span></span></span>

</div>

Critics focus on different weaknesses:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* ambiguity surrounding the 4 June launch records;
* the lack of direct documentary linkage to the ranch;
* reliance on retrospective reconstruction nearly fifty years after the event; and
* the absence of surviving physical evidence that can be conclusively identified as Flight 4.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeGovernment Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico &#124;...</span></span></span>

</div>

These criticisms do not automatically invalidate the [Mogul explanation]({{ 'mogul-explanation/' | relative_url }}), but they do affect the level of confidence that can reasonably be attached to identifying one specific launch.

## Overall assessment

Among all proposed Project Mogul candidates, Flight 4 remains the strongest and most widely cited explanation for the Roswell debris because it fits the chronology, the estimated flight path and the general characteristics of the recovered material. The Air Force considered the cumulative evidence sufficient to identify it as the probable source of the debris.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsa.gov">[NSA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report af roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORT OF AIR FORCE RESEARCH REGARDING THEMarch 24, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2018</span></span></span>

Nevertheless, the identification falls short of historical certainty. It depends on inference rather than direct documentation, and key confirming evidence—such as a contemporary recovery report, identifiable components or complete operational records—has never been found. For that reason, historians commonly describe Flight 4 not as a proven identification but as the **best-supported reconstruction available from the surviving evidence**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeGovernment Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico &#124;...</span></span></span>

    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
