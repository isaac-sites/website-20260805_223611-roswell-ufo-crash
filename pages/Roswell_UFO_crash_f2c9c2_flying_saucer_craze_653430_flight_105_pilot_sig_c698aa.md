## Introduction

The sighting involving United Air Lines Flight 105 on 4 July 1947 became one of the most influential episodes of the early flying saucer wave because it came from trained aviation professionals rather than casual observers. Just days after Kenneth Arnold's famous report near Mount Rainier, Captain Emil J. Smith, First Officer Ralph Stevens and stewardess Marty Morrow reported watching several flat, [unidentified]({{ 'unidentified/' | relative_url }}) objects flying in formation ahead of their aircraft for around 10 to 15 minutes over the Pacific Northwest. Although the observation did not establish what the objects were, it significantly shaped public opinion by demonstrating that experienced aircrew could also report unusual aerial phenomena. Contemporary newspapers, later investigators and modern historians alike have treated Flight 105 less as proof of extraordinary craft than as an important example of why pilot testimony carried unusual weight during the 1947 [flying saucer craze]({{ 'the-1940-s-flying/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

## What the crew reported over the Northwest

United Air Lines Flight 105, a Douglas DC-3 operating from Boise, Idaho, to Seattle, departed shortly after 9 p.m. local time on 4 July 1947. About eight minutes after take-off, while climbing through roughly 8,000 feet near Emmett, Idaho, co-pilot Ralph Stevens noticed a group of bright objects ahead of the aircraft. Captain Emil J. Smith initially assumed they were conventional aircraft and briefly illuminated the DC-3's landing lights as a precaution. Continued observation, however, convinced both pilots that the objects did not resemble ordinary aeroplanes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.project1947.com/fig/ual105.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[Project 1947]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project 1947PROJECT 1947: UAL Flight 105, July 4, 1947 - Capt E. J. Smith...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 4, 1947</span></span></span>

The pilots described five flattened or disc-like objects flying in a loose formation. Shortly afterwards, those objects disappeared and were replaced by another group, making a total of nine objects observed during the encounter. According to Smith, the objects appeared mostly as dark silhouettes against the twilight sky, making their exact shape difficult to determine. He avoided claiming they were perfectly circular, instead emphasising that they lacked visible wings or tails and maintained a formation unlike anything he recognised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

Rather than relying solely on their own impressions, the pilots summoned stewardess Marty Morrow into the cockpit. She independently confirmed seeing unusual objects ahead of the aircraft. The crew also radioed the Civil Aeronautics Administration station at Ontario, Oregon, and attempted to obtain confirmation from another United flight in the region. Neither ground personnel nor the other aircraft reported seeing the objects, leaving the Flight 105 crew as the only confirmed witnesses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

The sighting lasted considerably longer than many contemporary reports. Smith later estimated that the crew followed the objects for approximately 10 to 15 minutes, covering roughly 45 miles before the formation accelerated away or gradually disappeared from view.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

## Why pilot testimony impressed newspapers

During the first weeks of the 1947 saucer wave, newspapers published hundreds of reports from motorists, householders and curious members of the public. Many readers remained sceptical, assuming that excitement generated by Kenneth Arnold's report had encouraged exaggeration or misidentification.

Flight 105 altered that perception because its principal witnesses were experienced commercial aviators. Captain Emil J. Smith had accumulated thousands of flying hours and openly admitted that he had previously dismissed flying saucer stories. Contemporary accounts quoted him joking before departure that he would believe such reports only when he saw one himself. That prior scepticism became part of the story, making his subsequent report appear less likely to be motivated by enthusiasm for the phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.project1947.com/articles/flying750.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[Project 1947]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project 1947PROJECT 1947: FLYING Magazine: The Flying Saucers - Fact or Fiction? - July, 1950...</span></span></span>

Journalists also highlighted several aspects that distinguished the case from ordinary eyewitness accounts:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Multiple professional observers.** Two pilots independently observed the objects before inviting a third crew member to verify the sighting.
* **Extended viewing time.** Unlike a brief flash or distant light, the objects reportedly remained visible for several minutes.
* **Methodical reactions.** The crew attempted radio communication and sought independent confirmation instead of immediately concluding that the objects were extraordinary.
* **Measured descriptions.** Smith avoided dramatic claims, repeatedly acknowledging uncertainty about the exact shape and size of the objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

</div>

These characteristics made the incident one of the first pilot sightings to receive extensive national coverage. Aviation professionals were widely regarded as skilled observers trained to distinguish aircraft, weather conditions and navigational hazards, giving the report credibility that many civilian sightings lacked. Contemporary commentators later recalled that the Flight 105 report persuaded many previously sceptical readers that at least some witnesses were reporting genuine, unexplained observations rather than inventing stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

## What the sighting could and could not prove

The Flight 105 incident illustrates both the strengths and the limits of eyewitness [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}).

Its greatest strength lies in the quality of the observers. Commercial airline pilots routinely identify aircraft, estimate relative motion and monitor changing atmospheric conditions. Their testimony therefore deserves careful consideration. ## Flight 105 within the 1947 saucer wave

Within the broader context of [the 1947 flying]({{ 'the-1947-flying/' | relative_url }}) saucer craze that preceded the Roswell announcement, Flight 105 helped reinforce the perception that reports were not confined to inexperienced observers. Kenneth Arnold himself regarded the airline crew's testimony as particularly important because it appeared to validate that competent pilots could independently report similar aerial objects. Newspapers even photographed Arnold and Captain Smith comparing their experiences, reinforcing the impression that multiple aviation witnesses had encountered related phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_105_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Flight 105 UFO sighting</span></span></span>

For historians, the lasting importance of Flight 105 is therefore not that it resolved the mystery of the flying saucers. Instead, it demonstrates how professional witnesses influenced public debate.     var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
