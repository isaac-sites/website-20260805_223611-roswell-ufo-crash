## Introduction

[The Roswell UFO]({{ 'the-roswell-ufo/' | relative_url }}) crash is one of the most famous alleged extraterrestrial incidents in modern history. In July 1947, debris found on a ranch near Roswell, New Mexico, led the U.S. Army Air Forces to briefly announce that it had recovered a “flying disc” before quickly changing its explanation to a weather balloon. Decades later, the event became the centre of claims that the US government had recovered an alien spacecraft and hidden the evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>

## What happened in Roswell in 1947?

The incident began when rancher William “Mac” Brazel discovered unusual debris scattered across his property near Corona, New Mexico, roughly 75 miles from Roswell. The material included lightweight fragments, foil-like material, rubber and other components. Brazel reported the discovery to local authorities, who contacted [Roswell Army Air]({{ 'roswell-army-air/' | relative_url }}) Field (RAAF), home of the 509th Bombardment Group, the same unit associated with the atomic bomb missions of the Second World War.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>

On 8 July 1947, RAAF public information officer Walter Haut issued a statement saying personnel had recovered a “flying disc” from a ranch in the area. Newspapers quickly published the announcement, creating international attention at a time when reports of mysterious “flying saucers” were already spreading across the United States.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>

The military reversed course almost immediately. Brigadier General Roger Ramey of the Eighth Air Force displayed debris at a press conference in Fort Worth, Texas, and stated that the material came from a weather balloon and radar target rather than an unknown aircraft. The story largely disappeared from public attention for decades.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/rw/p/longviewnewsjournal9jul1947.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: Roswell 1947 - 1947 Press, Roswell explained, Texas sightings explained and unexplainedJuly 9, 1947...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 9, 1947</span></span></span>

## Why did Roswell become a UFO legend?

For many years, Roswell was not considered the defining UFO case it is today. Interest surged again in the late 1970s after researchers interviewed former military personnel who said the original explanation was incomplete. The release of books and documentaries claiming that a crashed alien spacecraft had been recovered transformed Roswell into the most famous UFO case in the world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/0611ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspaceforces.com">[Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspaceforces.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine USAF and the UFOs &#124; Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine USAF and the UFOs &#124; Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine</span></span></span>

Several factors helped the story grow:

* **The initial “flying disc” announcement:** The first military statement appeared to confirm that something unusual had been recovered, even though it was later withdrawn.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>
* **[Military secrecy]({{ 'military-secrecy/' | relative_url }}):** The US government was conducting highly classified aviation and nuclear-related programmes in the late 1940s. The presence of secret technology made a cover-up explanation seem plausible to some researchers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>
* **Changing witness accounts:** Some former personnel and local residents later described unusual materials or alleged recovered bodies, although these accounts appeared decades after the event and remain disputed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/The-Roswell-Report/quot/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Air Force The Roswell Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air Force The Roswell Report</span></span></span>
* **Popular culture:** Roswell became closely associated with alien imagery, appearing in films, television programmes, books and tourism campaigns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/news/articles/do-records-show-proof-of-ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Do Records Show Proof of UFOs? &#124; National Archives</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesDo Records Show Proof of UFOs? &#124; National ArchivesFebruary 9, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2018</span></span></span>

## The official explanation: Project Mogul

The US Air Force’s later investigations concluded that [the Roswell debris]({{ 'the-roswell-debris/' | relative_url }}) came from Project Mogul, a highly classified balloon programme intended to monitor Soviet nuclear activity by detecting distant sound waves from atomic explosions. The programme used large balloon trains carrying specialised equipment at high altitudes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>

The Air Force published its findings in *[The Roswell Report]({{ 'the-roswell-report/' | relative_url }}): Fact versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert* in 1995 after a congressional inquiry requested a search for records related to the alleged crash. The report concluded that the recovered material was associated with balloon research rather than an extraterrestrial craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D301-PURL-LPS71655" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: govinfo.gov">[GovInfo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">govinfo.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">GOVPUB D301 PURL LPS71655</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Roswell Report: Fact Versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert - GOVPUB-D301-PURL-LPS71655 &#124; Content Details &#124; GovInfoJanuary 1...</span></span></span>

This explanation also addressed later stories about alien bodies. The Air Force argued that some accounts had combined separate events involving high-altitude research, including later tests with anthropomorphic dummies, with memories of the 1947 incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/The-Roswell-Report/quot/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Air Force The Roswell Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air Force The Roswell Report</span></span></span>

## Why some researchers reject the official account

Supporters of the extraterrestrial interpretation argue that the official explanation does not answer every question surrounding Roswell. They point to several disputed issues:

* **The rapid change in the military story:** Critics argue that moving from “flying disc” to “weather balloon” within a day suggests officials were hiding something.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/0611ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspaceforces.com">[Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspaceforces.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine USAF and the UFOs &#124; Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine USAF and the UFOs &#124; Air &amp; Space Forces Magazine</span></span></span>
* **Witness descriptions:** Some later witnesses claimed the recovered material seemed unusual or unlike ordinary balloon debris.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaparchive.org/encounters/roswell-1947" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaparchive.org">[UAP Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaparchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Archive Roswell Incident — UAP Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Archive Roswell Incident — UAP Archive</span></span></span>
* **The body claims:** Some [UFO researchers]({{ 'ufo-investigators/' | relative_url }}) argue that reports of recovered bodies cannot be explained simply by Project Mogul, particularly because later dummy tests occurred after 1947.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaparchive.org/encounters/roswell-1947" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaparchive.org">[UAP Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaparchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Archive Roswell Incident — UAP Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Archive Roswell Incident — UAP Archive</span></span></span>

However, no publicly verified physical evidence of an extraterrestrial spacecraft or alien remains from Roswell has emerged. The strongest documented evidence remains consistent with a military balloon explanation, while the extraordinary claims rely mainly on later testimony, interpretation and disputed accounts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/The-Roswell-Report/quot/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Air Force The Roswell Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air Force The Roswell Report</span></span></span>

## Why Roswell still matters

Roswell remains influential because it sits at the intersection of several powerful themes: government secrecy, military technology, [public trust]({{ 'public-trust/' | relative_url }}) and humanity’s fascination with the possibility of life beyond Earth. The incident occurred during a period when real secret programmes were common, including nuclear research and experimental aviation, making the boundary between genuine mystery and speculation especially difficult for the public to judge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/reports-ufos-1947-roswell-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: airandspace.si.edu">[National Air and Space Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">airandspace.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">reports ufos 1947 roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space MuseumReports of UFOs: 1947 Roswell Incident &#124; National Air and Space MuseumOctober 20, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 20, 2016</span></span></span>

Whether viewed as a misunderstood military experiment or a possible extraterrestrial event, Roswell has become a landmark case in the history of UFO beliefs. Its lasting importance comes less from proven evidence of alien visitation and more from the way one confusing episode developed into a global symbol of the search for answers about what may exist beyond Earth.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/news/articles/do-records-show-proof-of-ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Do Records Show Proof of UFOs? &#124; National Archives</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesDo Records Show Proof of UFOs? &#124; National ArchivesFebruary 9, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2018</span></span></span>

      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
