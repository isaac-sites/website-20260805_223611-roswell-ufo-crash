## Introduction

Roswell's [annual UFO Festival]({{ 'annual-ufo-festival/' | relative_url }}) is unusual because it has no single entrance gate, no universal ticket and no reliable turnstile count. Visitors move freely between city streets, parks, museums, concerts, shops and independently organised attractions. As a result, the city's published attendance and spending figures are estimates rather than direct headcounts. Instead of relying on one measurement, Roswell combines several indirect indicators—including ticket sales for paid events, hotel occupancy, visitor surveys, sanitation estimates and tourism analytics—to build a picture of how many people attended and how much economic activity the festival generated. This approach produces useful planning data, but it also means that headline attendance figures should be understood as informed estimates with important limitations rather than precise census counts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why free city-wide events resist precise counting

Unlike a stadium concert or ticketed exhibition, [the Roswell UFO]({{ 'the-roswell-ufo/' | relative_url }}) Festival is spread across numerous public spaces. Visitors may attend a parade, browse vendors, watch concerts, visit museums or simply walk through the downtown area without ever registering through a single entry point.

This creates several measurement problems:

Instead, the published total represents a synthesis of multiple datasets intended to estimate unique visitors and their economic contribution rather than a literal count of everyone present at every event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

## Which indirect indicators Roswell uses

The city's 2023 festival report describes a methodology that combines several independent sources instead of relying on any single metric. Each captures a different aspect of visitor activity.

### Visitor surveys

On-site surveys collect information including where visitors travelled from, how long they stayed, where they lodged and what they spent on accommodation, food, shopping and entertainment.

These responses help distinguish local residents from out-of-town visitors and provide average spending estimates that can be projected across the broader visitor population. Survey-based spending estimates are common in tourism studies but depend on representative sampling and accurate self-reporting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

### Ticket sales

Although much of the festival is free, some events require admission. Ticketed concerts and other paid attractions provide verified attendance figures for those specific activities.

These numbers are among the most reliable components of the overall estimate, but they represent only a portion of total festival participation because many visitors never purchase tickets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

### Hotel occupancy

Accommodation data provides one of the strongest indicators of tourism demand.

Hotel occupancy rates, room nights and lodging information help estimate how many visitors stayed overnight rather than making day trips. Because overnight visitors generally spend more on restaurants, shopping and fuel, these figures also improve economic impact calculations. Roswell separately tracks lodgers' tax performance as another indicator of visitor activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

### Sanitation estimates

One of the more unusual inputs is sanitation data.

The city uses refuse collection and related operational measurements to estimate crowd levels in heavily used public areas. While rubbish generation cannot identify individual visitors, it offers an operational proxy for the intensity of public use during large unticketed events. This method is particularly useful where people circulate continuously through open streets rather than entering a controlled venue.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

### Tourism analytics

The 2023 methodology also incorporates digital visitor analytics through tourism tracking tools identified in the report as Visit Widget, together with website and social-media analytics.

These datasets help estimate visitor origins and engagement patterns, supplementing traditional survey and accommodation information. ## How reliable are the published figures?

Roswell's approach reflects a common challenge in measuring open, city-wide festivals. No indirect indicator is sufficient on its own:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Surveys** provide spending information but depend on sampling quality.
* **Hotel occupancy** captures overnight tourism but misses many day visitors.
* **Ticket sales** are precise but cover only paid events.
* **Sanitation measures** reflect crowd intensity rather than unique individuals.
* **Digital tourism analytics** reveal visitor patterns but cannot independently verify attendance.

</div>

The strength of the system lies in combining these datasets rather than treating any one of them as definitive. Even so, published attendance and spending figures should be viewed as planning estimates designed to inform tourism policy, budgeting and marketing, not as exact population counts. The methodology is transparent about its reliance on indirect evidence, which is generally more informative than presenting a single attendance figure without explaining how it was derived.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO FESTIVALFebruary 18, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2026</span></span></span>

  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
