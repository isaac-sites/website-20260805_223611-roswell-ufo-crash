## Introduction

Walter Haut's posthumous affidavit is one of the most significant late additions to [the Roswell cover-up]({{ 'the-roswell-cover-up/' | relative_url }}) narrative because it came from the officer who issued the famous 8 July 1947 press release announcing that the Roswell Army Air Field had recovered a "flying disc". For decades, Haut publicly described his role as largely administrative: he prepared and distributed the statement on the orders of his commanding officer, Colonel William Blanchard, but did not claim first-hand knowledge of an alien craft or bodies. His sealed affidavit, signed in 2002 and released only after his death in 2005, fundamentally expanded that account by asserting that he had personally seen an unusual craft and non-human bodies. Those claims immediately became central to [Roswell believers]({{ 'believers-and-sceptics/' | relative_url }}), while historians and sceptics argued that their late appearance and the impossibility of cross-examining Haut after his death make them difficult to evaluate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wonderfulmuseums.com/museum/roswell-history-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wonderfulmuseums.com">[Wonderful Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wonderfulmuseums.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wonderful MuseumsRoswell History Museum: Unearthing the Past and the Unexplained in New Mexico - Wonderful MuseumsSeptember 7, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 7, 2025</span></span></span>

## Haut's documented role in the 1947 announcement

Walter Haut's documented involvement in the original Roswell incident is unusually well established. As public information officer at Roswell Army Air Field, he prepared and released the press statement announcing that personnel from the 509th Bomb Group had recovered a "flying disc" from a local ranch. The release was issued on Colonel Blanchard's authority rather than as Haut's independent assessment.

This distinction matters because contemporary records demonstrate Haut's official function, but they do not show that he participated in debris recovery or scientific examination. For many years after the incident, Haut consistently described himself as the officer who handled public communications rather than as a direct witness to extraordinary evidence. He later helped establish the International UFO Museum in Roswell and remained associated with Roswell research, but his public statements generally stopped short of claiming that he had personally viewed alien bodies or an extraterrestrial vehicle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wired.com/story/roswell-aliens-fermi-paradox" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wired.com">[WIRED]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wired.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Here&#x27;s the Proof There&#x27;s No Government Alien Conspiracy Around Roswell</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">However, an examination reveals a confluence of secret government projects and Cold War era activities rather than extraterrestrial invol...</span></span></span>

The contrast between this long public position and the much broader claims contained in his final affidavit is one reason the document has attracted sustained attention.

## What new claims appeared in the 2002 affidavit?

The affidavit, signed in December 2002 and notarised with instructions that it remain sealed until after Haut's death, introduced several claims that went well beyond anything he had publicly asserted before.

According to the affidavit, Haut claimed that:

* Colonel [Blanchard]({{ 'blanchard/' | relative_url }}) personally escorted him to a heavily guarded hangar on the Roswell base.

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Inside, he observed an egg-shaped craft approximately 12 to 15 feet long with no obvious propulsion system, wings or landing gear.
* He saw several small bodies near the craft, which he described as apparently non-human.
* Senior officers deliberately restricted access and tightly controlled information.
* The weather-balloon explanation was knowingly false and intended to conceal the true recovery.

</div>

These statements transformed Haut from the officer who announced the original story into an alleged first-hand witness to both the craft and its occupants. Researchers Thomas Carey and Donald Schmitt later published the affidavit and treated it as an important insider account supporting the extraterrestrial interpretation of Roswell.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.apeirron.com/node/roswell" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apeirron.com">[Apeirron]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apeirron.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Roswell Incident — Apeirron</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Roswell Incident — Apeirron...</span></span></span>

## Why the affidavit became so influential

Within Roswell literature, the affidavit carries unusual weight because of Haut's proximity to the original events. Unlike many later witnesses, his presence at Roswell Army Air Field on 8 July 1947 is undisputed, and his role in issuing the famous press release is documented.

Supporters argue that several features enhance its credibility:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Haut signed the statement under oath before a notary.
* He requested that it remain sealed until after his death, which supporters interpret as reducing personal incentives for publicity.
* The affidavit appears broadly consistent with other late witness accounts alleging recovered craft and bodies.
* As the base's public information officer, he was well positioned to know whether the initial press release reflected genuine military belief.

</div>

For believers, the affidavit therefore appears to explain why the original "[flying disc]({{ 'flying-disc/' | relative_url }})" announcement was issued before the rapid reversal later that day.

## Why historians remain cautious

The affidavit also illustrates many of the methodological problems associated with late witness testimony.

Most importantly, Haut never publicly defended or elaborated on these new claims after their release because the affidavit became public only after his death in 2005. Historians therefore cannot ask follow-up questions, probe inconsistencies, clarify ambiguous passages or test his recollections against later interviews.

Other factors also complicate assessment:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The affidavit appeared roughly fifty-five years after the events it describes, raising well-known concerns about long-term memory.
* Several dramatic details—including personal observation of bodies—had not featured prominently in Haut's earlier public accounts.
* Researchers cannot determine precisely how much discussion with Roswell investigators or exposure to decades of published Roswell narratives may have influenced later recollections.
* Some descriptions overlap with claims made by other late Roswell witnesses, making it difficult to distinguish independent memory from shared narrative development.

</div>

These issues do not prove the affidavit false, but they substantially limit its evidential value compared with contemporary documents created in 1947.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/The-Roswell-Report/quot/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force The Roswell Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air Force The Roswell Report</span></span></span>

## How the affidavit compares with the historical record

The affidavit occupies an unusual position within Roswell evidence because it conflicts with the surviving contemporary documentary record while relying almost entirely on retrospective personal testimony.

The surviving military documents from July 1947 establish that Haut issued the press release and that higher headquarters rapidly replaced the "flying disc" story with a balloon explanation. However, no surviving records independently corroborate Haut's later description of viewing an egg-shaped craft or non-human bodies.

Subsequent US Air Force investigations concluded that the Roswell debris was associated with the classified [Project Mogul balloon]({{ 'project-mogul-balloon/' | relative_url }}) programme and argued that later body stories resulted from the blending of unrelated Cold War activities—including anthropomorphic dummy tests and aircraft accidents—with memories of the original recovery. ## What the affidavit ultimately changes

Walter Haut's final affidavit unquestionably changed the Roswell debate by adding detailed first-person claims from the individual responsible for the original "flying disc" announcement. Before its publication, Haut's documented role centred on military public affairs. Afterwards, he became one of the principal witnesses cited in support of claims that Roswell involved the recovery of an extraterrestrial craft and its occupants.

At the same time, the affidavit's evidential limitations are equally clear. Because the testimony surfaced only after Haut's death, its most consequential assertions cannot be examined through questioning, corroborated by contemporaneous documentation or tested against additional explanation from the witness himself. As a result, the affidavit remains one of the most influential—but also one of the most difficult to verify—pieces of evidence within the broader Roswell cover-up narrative.

    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
