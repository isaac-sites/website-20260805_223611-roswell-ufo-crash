## Introduction

Many visitors arrive in Roswell expecting to find a clearly marked crash site close to the city's UFO attractions. In reality, that is not how the Roswell story is organised geographically. The area where rancher W. W. "Mac" Brazel reported finding unusual debris in July 1947 lies on remote ranchland and nearby public land roughly northwest of Roswell, far from the downtown museums and souvenir shops. Access has long been complicated by private property, limited public rights of way and disagreement over which locations are historically significant. As a result, visiting the "Roswell crash site" is not a straightforward tourist excursion but a journey into a sparsely populated landscape where history, land ownership and competing interpretations overlap.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blm.gov/visit/alleged-ufo-skip-site" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blm.gov">[Bureau of Land Management]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bureau of Land Management Alleged UFO Skip Site</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bureau of Land ManagementAlleged UFO Skip Site - New Mexico &#124; Bureau of Land Management...</span></span></span>

## Where was the original debris reported?

The best-documented part of the 1947 incident is not a dramatic crater but a large debris field discovered on grazing land associated with the Foster Ranch, where Brazel worked. Contemporary military accounts and later investigations describe scattered lightweight material spread across a broad area rather than a single impact point. This distinction matters because many visitors expect one identifiable location when the historical record instead points to an extensive debris field.

Over time, researchers have proposed several related locations, including a separate "impact" or "skip" site where believers argue an object first struck the ground before continuing elsewhere. These locations are not universally accepted, even within UFO research, and different books, maps and investigators have identified different coordinates. That continuing disagreement is one reason no single place has become an undisputed heritage site.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blm.gov/visit/alleged-ufo-skip-site" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blm.gov">[Bureau of Land Management]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bureau of Land Management Alleged UFO Skip Site</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bureau of Land ManagementAlleged UFO Skip Site - New Mexico &#124; Bureau of Land Management...</span></span></span>

## Why downtown Roswell is the main visitor destination

For most travellers, the practical Roswell experience is centred on the city rather than the ranchlands.

The International UFO Museum and Research Center presents documents, witness accounts, models and competing interpretations in a location that is easy to reach. It offers historical context without requiring visitors to navigate remote roads or negotiate access across private property. The museum therefore functions as an interpretive centre rather than a gateway to a preserved archaeological site.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/hours-of-operation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hours of Operation &#124; International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hours of Operation &#124; International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

This reflects a broader reality of [Roswell tourism]({{ 'roswell-tourism/' | relative_url }}). The city's attractions are designed to explain and commemorate the story, while the places associated with the original events remain largely undeveloped rural landscapes. Unlike famous battlefields or national parks, there is no officially preserved "Roswell crash site" with visitor facilities, guided paths and permanent interpretation.

## Why access has always been difficult

Several practical factors have prevented the reported debris area from becoming an ordinary tourist attraction.


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* **Private ranchland:** Much of the landscape associated with the 1947 recovery lies on or adjacent to privately owned ranches. Landowners control access, and permission cannot be assumed.
* **Remote terrain:** The area is many miles from Roswell across sparsely populated country with limited services and long driving distances.
* **Road limitations:** Historic routes often cross private gates or ranch tracks rather than public visitor roads.
* **No preserved historical complex:** Unlike many heritage destinations, there is no government-managed visitor centre marking the original debris discovery area.

</div>

## Public access is changing—but only in a limited way

In recent years, the U.S. Bureau of Land Management (BLM) has taken steps to provide lawful public access to one location it calls the **Alleged UFO Skip Site** near Corona, New Mexico. The agency has established a parking area and directs visitors to reach the location on foot via roughly a one-mile hike across public land. Vehicles are not permitted beyond the parking area, partly because roads leading directly to the location are blocked by surrounding private property.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blm.gov/visit/alleged-ufo-skip-site" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blm.gov">[Bureau of Land Management]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bureau of Land Management Alleged UFO Skip Site</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bureau of Land ManagementAlleged UFO Skip Site - New Mexico &#124; Bureau of Land Management...</span></span></span>

Importantly, the BLM does not present the site as confirmed [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) of an extraterrestrial crash. Instead, it manages the area as a place associated with a famous historical controversy. Even the official name—"Alleged UFO Skip Site"—signals that the agency is acknowledging the story's cultural significance without endorsing any particular explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blm.gov/visit/alleged-ufo-skip-site" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blm.gov">[Bureau of Land Management]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bureau of Land Management Alleged UFO Skip Site</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bureau of Land ManagementAlleged UFO Skip Site - New Mexico &#124; Bureau of Land Management...</span></span></span>

## Why there are so many competing "crash sites"

One source of confusion is that different authors use the phrase "Roswell crash site" to describe different places.

Some refer to the original debris field where Brazel found scattered material. Others describe an alleged skip site. Still others discuss a separate impact location proposed in later witness testimony or UFO investigations. ## What visitors should realistically expect

Anyone hoping to combine Roswell's museums with a visit to the reported debris area should plan for a very different experience from typical sightseeing.

The landscape is open ranch country rather than a developed attraction. Visitors should expect long drives, limited mobile coverage in some areas, few amenities and the need to respect property boundaries. Even where public access exists, the site itself is intentionally modest: there are no reconstructed spacecraft, visitor buildings or dramatic visual landmarks.

That contrast is part of what makes Roswell unusual. The city's museums and themed downtown provide the narrative, while the places connected to the 1947 reports remain quiet, remote and only partially accessible. For many visitors, understanding that distinction is essential to setting realistic expectations before making the journey.

    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
