## Introduction

Roswell's [annual UFO Festival]({{ 'annual-ufo-festival/' | relative_url }}) exists primarily as an economic development strategy rather than as an official commemoration of the disputed 1947 [Roswell incident]({{ 'the-1980-book/' | relative_url }}). When the City of Roswell launched the event in 1995, local leaders recognised that worldwide curiosity about UFOs had become a marketable tourism asset. Instead of trying to settle debates over what happened in 1947, the city chose to encourage visitors to stay longer, spend more with local businesses and reinforce Roswell's reputation as the world's best-known "alien" destination. Over time, the festival has become one of Roswell's most important tourism initiatives, supporting hotels, restaurants, retailers, museums and event organisers while strengthening a year-round visitor economy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1757/UFO-Festival-Report-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Festival Report 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO Festival Report 2022 &#124; Roswell, NM...</span></span></span>

## Why the city launched the festival in 1995

By the early 1990s, Roswell already possessed an internationally recognised brand because of the alleged UFO crash. Television programmes, books and popular culture had transformed the city's name into shorthand for extraterrestrial mysteries. Local officials faced a practical question: how could that attention generate sustained economic activity rather than occasional curiosity?

The answer was to create an annual destination event. Rather than presenting the festival as an official endorsement of any explanation for the 1947 incident, the city framed it as a tourism attraction capable of drawing visitors during the summer travel season. This complemented the opening of the International UFO Museum and Research Center a few years earlier, giving Roswell both a permanent attraction and a signature annual event. The two reinforce one another: the museum encourages year-round tourism, while the festival produces a concentrated surge in visitors and international publicity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1757/UFO-Festival-Report-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Festival Report 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO Festival Report 2022 &#124; Roswell, NM...</span></span></span>

The economic objective has remained explicit in city publications. Official festival reports describe the event in terms of visitor numbers, direct economic impact, hotel occupancy and business participation, demonstrating that municipal success is measured by tourism performance rather than by any scientific conclusions about the Roswell incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1757/UFO-Festival-Report-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Festival Report 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO Festival Report 2022 &#124; Roswell, NM...</span></span></span>

## Where visitor spending flows during the weekend

The festival is designed so that spending is distributed across the local economy rather than concentrated in a single venue.

Visitors typically spend money on:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Hotel and motel accommodation, particularly during the busiest anniversary years.
* Restaurants, cafés, breweries and themed dining events such as the Alien Crawl.
* Museum admissions and guided tours.
* Souvenir shops selling alien-themed merchandise.
* Festival vendors, artists and craft businesses.
* Entertainment, concerts and family activities organised throughout the city.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1757/UFO-Festival-Report-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Festival Report 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO Festival Report 2022 &#124; Roswell, NM...</span></span></span>

</div>

This broad distribution matters because tourism has a multiplier effect. A visitor who books a hotel room is also likely to eat locally, purchase souvenirs, visit museums and use other services. As a result, the festival benefits businesses that have little direct connection to [UFO culture]({{ 'roswell-and-ufo/' | relative_url }}) but still profit from increased foot traffic.

City reports illustrate how this spending can fluctuate with the scale of the event. The 75th-anniversary festival in 2022 generated an estimated direct economic impact of approximately **$2.19 million** and attracted more than **40,000 visitors**, while the smaller 2023 festival generated roughly **$510,000** in direct economic impact and just over **3,300 visitors**, reflecting a more modest programme after the exceptional anniversary year.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1757/UFO-Festival-Report-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Festival Report 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMUFO Festival Report 2022 &#124; Roswell, NM...</span></span></span>

## How public investment supports year-round tourism

Roswell does not simply stage the festival and hope visitors arrive. The city maintains an institutional framework that uses tourism revenue to support continued promotion.

A central mechanism is the municipal **Lodgers' Tax**, a five-percent occupancy tax collected from hotels and other accommodation providers. Under city policy, these revenues are used to promote tourism-related events, advertise Roswell as a visitor destination and support facilities that attract overnight guests. The system effectively channels spending by tourists into future tourism marketing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1030/Lodgers-Tax-and-Special-Events-Informati" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NMLodgers&#x27; Tax and Special Events Information &#124; Roswell, NM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMLodgers&#x27; Tax and Special Events Information &#124; Roswell, NM</span></span></span>

Applications for Lodgers' Tax funding are evaluated partly on their ability to attract visitors from outside the local community and increase hotel bookings. This illustrates that the city's investment decisions focus on measurable tourism outcomes rather than cultural prestige alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1398/Occupancy-Tax-Board" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NMOccupancy Tax Board &#124; Roswell, NM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMOccupancy Tax Board &#124; Roswell, NM</span></span></span>

## Keeping the alien identity alive beyond one weekend

Although the UFO Festival lasts only a few days, its economic purpose extends across the entire year.
