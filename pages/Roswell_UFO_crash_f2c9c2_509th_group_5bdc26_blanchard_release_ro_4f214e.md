## Introduction

Colonel William H. Blanchard occupied the pivotal command position when [Roswell Army Air]({{ 'roswell-army-air/' | relative_url }}) Field issued its famous 8 July 1947 announcement that the 509th Bombardment Group had recovered a “[flying disc]({{ 'flying-disc/' | relative_url }})”. Although his authority over the base is not in dispute, the surviving historical record does not conclusively document the precise decision-making process that led to the release. What is well established is that the statement originated from his command, that public information officer First Lieutenant Walter Haut distributed it on his authority, and that higher headquarters reversed the announcement within hours by identifying the recovered material as a balloon and radar reflector. The central historical question is therefore not whether Blanchard was responsible for the command under which the release was issued, but how much he personally knew, approved and intended when authorising one of the most famous military press statements in modern history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/roswell-ufo-aliens-what-happened" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">roswell ufo aliens what happened</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What Really Happened at Roswell? &#124; HISTORYDecember 18, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 18, 2019</span></span></span>

## Blanchard's command authority at Roswell

In July 1947, William H. Blanchard commanded Roswell Army Air Field and the 509th Bombardment Group, then the United States' only operational atomic-capable bombardment group. As base commander, he had authority over intelligence activities, public information and communications leaving the installation. Consequently, the press release cannot be understood as an unauthorised act by junior personnel. It emerged from the base command structure for which Blanchard was ultimately responsible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/roswell-ufo-aliens-what-happened" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">roswell ufo aliens what happened</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What Really Happened at Roswell? &#124; HISTORYDecember 18, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 18, 2019</span></span></span>

The sequence reconstructed from contemporary accounts is broadly consistent across historical sources. Rancher W. W. "Mac" Brazel reported unusual debris through Sheriff George Wilcox. Intelligence officer Major Jesse Marcel investigated and recovered material. The information reached Blanchard, after which public information officer Walter Haut prepared and released the now-famous statement announcing possession of a "flying disc". Within hours, Brigadier General Roger Ramey at Eighth Air Force headquarters in Fort Worth publicly replaced that account with the balloon explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/roswell-ufo-aliens-what-happened" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[history.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">roswell ufo aliens what happened</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What Really Happened at Roswell? &#124; HISTORYDecember 18, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 18, 2019</span></span></span>

This chain of events shows that Blanchard stood at the key junction between the intelligence officers examining the debris and the public announcement that followed. It does not, however, establish exactly what assessment he had received regarding the material before approving publicity.

## What is known about the release approval

The strongest [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) that Blanchard authorised the announcement comes from Walter Haut, whose role was to communicate official information rather than formulate policy independently. In later interviews, Haut consistently stated that he issued the press release because Blanchard instructed him to do so. The wording itself presented the recovery of a "flying disc" as an accomplished fact rather than as an unverified possibility, making the announcement unusually categorical for a military public information release.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.prweek.com/article/1232280/analysis-history-pr-ufo-euphoria-invades-roswell-52-years-the-power-pr-a-press-release-fuels-fire-existence-flying-saucers-sherri-deatherage-green" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prweek.com">[PRWeek]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prweek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ANALYSIS: History of PR</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ANALYSIS: History of PR - UFO euphoria invades Roswell for 52 years/THE POWER OF PR/A press release fuels the fire over the existen...</span></span></span>

Even so, historians distinguish between two separate questions:

* **Whether Blanchard authorised the release.** The available evidence strongly supports this.
* **Why he authorised it.** The surviving documentary record does not provide a definitive answer.

Several possibilities have been proposed over the decades. Some writers argue that Blanchard believed the intelligence officers had genuinely recovered something unlike any conventional aircraft debris they recognised. Others suggest he may have expected higher headquarters to review the matter before widespread publication, while another interpretation is that the release reflected confusion during the intense "flying saucer" wave of summer 1947 rather than a carefully considered institutional conclusion. None of these explanations can presently be demonstrated conclusively from surviving official records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theoriesofanything.com/research/roswell-incident-historical-analysis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theoriesofanything.com">[Theories of Anything]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theoriesofanything.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on theoriesofanything.com.</span></span></span>

Importantly, there is no surviving written order from Blanchard explaining his reasoning, nor has any authenticated memorandum been found describing the internal discussion that preceded the release.

## What his temporary absence does and does not prove

One frequently discussed detail is that Blanchard was reportedly away from Roswell Army Air Field for part of the day after the announcement had been issued. This has sometimes been interpreted as evidence that he neither supervised nor fully understood the release.

The available evidence does not support such a strong conclusion.

Being temporarily absent after authorising a statement is not unusual for a base commander and does not imply that subordinates acted without authority. The more important point is that the release had already been issued through the command chain before higher headquarters intervened. Whether Blanchard remained physically present during the rapidly developing media response has little bearing on whether the decision originated under his command.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.apeirron.com/node/roswell" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apeirron.com">[Apeirron]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apeirron.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Roswell Incident — Apeirron</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Roswell Incident — Apeirron...</span></span></span>

At the same time, his absence also cannot be used to demonstrate that others independently chose the wording or that he had no responsibility for it. The evidence simply does not support either extreme interpretation.

## Why the decision remains historically uncertain

The enduring uncertainty arises because the documentary trail is incomplete.

Several factors complicate reconstruction of the approval process:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* No contemporaneous explanation by Blanchard survives describing why he believed the release was justified.
* Later recollections by participants were recorded decades after the event and sometimes differ in important details.
* Official attention shifted almost immediately to General Roger Ramey's balloon explanation, leaving little documentation of internal discussion at Roswell itself.
* Later Air Force investigations focused primarily on identifying the recovered material—ultimately attributing it to Project Mogul—rather than reconstructing the precise internal approval process behind the original press release.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theoriesofanything.com/research/roswell-incident-historical-analysis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theoriesofanything.com">[Theories of Anything]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theoriesofanything.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on theoriesofanything.com.</span></span></span>

</div>

These gaps explain why historians generally distinguish carefully between established facts and later speculation. The existence of the release, Blanchard's command position and Haut's role are well documented. The motivations behind the decision remain less certain.

## Assessing Blanchard's responsibility

A balanced historical assessment separates command responsibility from broader claims about the [Roswell incident]({{ 'the-1980-book/' | relative_url }}).

The available evidence supports several conclusions with reasonable [confidence]({{ 'confidence/' | relative_url }}):


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Blanchard was the commanding officer responsible for Roswell Army Air Field and the 509th Bombardment Group.
* The "flying disc" announcement was issued through his command.
* Walter Haut stated that he released the announcement under Blanchard's instructions.
* Higher headquarters rapidly superseded the announcement with the balloon explanation.

</div>

Beyond those points, the evidence becomes increasingly uncertain. The surviving record does not demonstrate that Blanchard knowingly announced the recovery of an extraterrestrial craft, nor does it prove that he intentionally disseminated false information. Likewise, there is no documentary evidence showing that the press release was issued without his approval.

For that reason, Blanchard's historical significance lies less in resolving what [the Roswell debris]({{ 'the-roswell-debris/' | relative_url }}) actually was than in explaining how one of the most consequential military press releases of the twentieth century emerged from the command of the 509th Bombardment Group before being publicly reversed only hours later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/roswell-ufo-aliens-what-happened" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[history.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">roswell ufo aliens what happened</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What Really Happened at Roswell? &#124; HISTORYDecember 18, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 18, 2019</span></span></span>

    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
