## Introduction

Each summer, Roswell's [annual UFO Festival]({{ 'annual-ufo-festival/' | relative_url }}) turns the city's historic centre into a large-scale public celebration where the story of the 1947 [Roswell incident]({{ 'the-1980-book/' | relative_url }}) becomes something people actively participate in rather than simply read about. For several days, streets normally used for everyday commerce are filled with costume contests, parades, themed markets, live entertainment, educational talks and family activities. The result is a festival that appeals simultaneously to committed UFO researchers, science-fiction fans, curious tourists, sceptics and local residents. Rather than asking visitors to decide whether the Roswell story is true, the festival invites them to experience how the mystery has shaped the city's identity and economy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/upcoming-events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">upcoming events</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2026 UFO Museum UFOlogist Invasion &#124; InternationalUFO Museum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

## Festival events turn the city into an interactive attraction

Unlike a museum visit, which is centred on exhibitions and interpretation, the UFO Festival spreads activities across downtown Roswell. Main Street and surrounding blocks become venues for entertainment, shopping and public gatherings, creating an atmosphere closer to a street carnival than a historical commemoration.

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Alien-themed costume competitions and cosplay.
* Street parades featuring illuminated vehicles, performers and community groups.
* Live music and outdoor entertainment.

Instead of concentrating visitors in a single building, organisers deliberately distribute attractions throughout the downtown district. Walking between venues becomes part of the experience, encouraging people to browse shops, stop at cafés and interact with street performers.

The festival also overlaps with programming at the International UFO Museum and Research Center, where researchers, authors and long-time investigators deliver lectures and panel discussions. These talks give visitors interested in the history of the Roswell incident an opportunity to hear competing interpretations while the surrounding streets maintain a more playful atmosphere.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/upcoming-events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">upcoming events</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2026 UFO Museum UFOlogist Invasion &#124; InternationalUFO Museum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

## Believers, sceptics and families share the same celebration

One of the festival's distinctive qualities is that it welcomes people with very different reasons for attending.

Some visitors travel specifically to hear recognised [UFO researchers]({{ 'ufo-investigators/' | relative_url }}) discuss witness testimony, government documents and new interpretations of historical [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}). Others attend because they enjoy science fiction, costumes or unusual festivals. Many families simply treat the event as a holiday celebration with entertainment suitable for children.

This mixture produces an unusually broad audience:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* UFO enthusiasts seeking lectures and discussions.
* Sceptics interested in the cultural phenomenon rather than extraterrestrial claims.
* Cosplayers and popular culture fans.
* Families attracted by games, parades and performances.
* Travellers looking for an unusual American festival experience.

</div>

Rather than forcing agreement on what happened in 1947, the event accommodates multiple viewpoints. Serious discussion can take place inside conference rooms while, only a short walk away, children pose with costumed aliens or visitors compete in themed costume contests. That coexistence has become one of the festival's defining characteristics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/upcoming-events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">upcoming events</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2026 UFO Museum UFOlogist Invasion &#124; InternationalUFO Museum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

## Downtown businesses embrace the alien theme

The festival creates one of the busiest trading periods of the year for many downtown businesses.

Restaurants extend opening hours, retailers stock alien-themed merchandise and temporary vendors fill streets with souvenirs, artwork, clothing and food. Official festival organisers actively recruit both commercial vendors and food trucks, requiring continuous operation during festival hours and allocating booth locations throughout downtown to maximise visitor movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufofestival.com/vendors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufofestival.com">[ufofestival.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufofestival.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufofestival.com.</span></span></span>

The commercial impact extends beyond souvenir sales. Hotels, cafés, bars and local attractions all benefit from increased visitor numbers, while businesses often decorate storefronts with extraterrestrial themes to become part of the festival itself rather than merely serving nearby customers.

Because visitors spend much of the day walking between events, the entire downtown retail district functions as part of the attraction instead of acting only as supporting infrastructure.

## A citywide partnership rather than a single event

The UFO Festival is organised as a collaborative civic event rather than solely as a museum programme. MainStreet Roswell, the City of Roswell, local businesses and cultural organisations all contribute to planning activities, logistics and visitor services.

Official planning documents describe the festival as a signature tourism event that generates visitor spending while promoting Roswell's identity. Festival operations require street closures, vendor management, public safety coordination and infrastructure capable of supporting thousands of visitors over several days.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_02032026-2981" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NMFINANCE COMMITTEE AGENDAJanuary 30, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 30, 2026</span></span></span>

## More than a festival about the crash

Although the Roswell incident provides the historical inspiration, the festival's success depends less on resolving the mystery than on transforming it into a shared civic experience.

Visitors do not simply observe exhibits about an alleged crash. They dress as aliens, attend lectures, watch parades, browse themed markets and encounter UFO imagery throughout the streets. In doing so, they temporarily become participants in Roswell's most recognisable cultural tradition.

That transformation explains why the festival remains central to Roswell's tourism economy.     if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
