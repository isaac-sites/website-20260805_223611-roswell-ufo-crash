## Introduction

One of the most persistent historical errors in accounts of the [Roswell incident]({{ 'the-1980-book/' | relative_url }}) is referring to the military organisation involved as the **509th Bombardment Wing**. In fact, the unit stationed at [Roswell Army Air]({{ 'roswell-army-air/' | relative_url }}) Field during the July 1947 debris recovery was the **509th Bombardment Group, Very Heavy**. The **509th Bombardment Wing** did not become an operational headquarters until **17 November 1947**, more than four months after the incident. Although the distinction may appear minor, it affects the accuracy of military records, organisational history and later interpretations of the Roswell story. Official Air Force lineage documents clearly distinguish between the two formations and their respective dates of activation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/434118/509-bomb-wing-acc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force509 Bomb Wing (ACC) &gt; Air Force &gt; Fact Sheet Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 3, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 2015</span></span></span>

## Was It the 509th Group or Wing?

The answer is straightforward: in **July 1947**, the organisation responsible for responding to the reported debris near Roswell was the **509th Bombardment Group**.

At the time, Roswell Army Air Field's flying units, intelligence staff and command structure all belonged to the group. Colonel William H. [Blanchard]({{ 'blanchard/' | relative_url }}) commanded the 509th Bombardment Group, while intelligence officer Major Jesse A. Marcel served within its headquarters. The famous press release announcing the recovery of a "[flying disc]({{ 'flying-disc/' | relative_url }})" was issued through the group's command structure because there was no 509th Bombardment Wing yet in existence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.whiteman.af.mil/News/Commentaries/Display/Article/325353/historian-documents-509th-whiteman-afb-significance/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whiteman.af.mil">[Whiteman Air Force Base]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whiteman.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Whiteman Air Force BaseHistorian documents 509th, Whiteman AFB significance &gt; Whiteman Air Force Base &gt; NewsApril 12, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2012</span></span></span>

Military organisations frequently retain the same numerical designation while changing structure over time. In this case, the number "509th" remained constant, but the organisational level changed from a **group** to a **wing** as the newly independent Air Force reorganised its strategic bomber forces during late 1947 and the years that followed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/434118/509-bomb-wing-acc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force509 Bomb Wing (ACC) &gt; Air Force &gt; Fact Sheet Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 3, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 2015</span></span></span>

## The Unit Structure in July 1947

Understanding the terminology helps explain why the confusion developed.

In the United States Army Air Forces of 1947:

* A **bombardment group** was the primary operational organisation, typically controlling several bomber squadrons.
* A **wing** was a larger headquarters intended to supervise one or more operational groups together with support organisations.

During the Roswell incident, the 509th Bombardment Group was itself the senior operational formation at the base. There was no higher 509th wing headquarters above it. Consequently, official reports, personnel assignments and contemporary documents consistently identify the responding organisation as the **509th Bombardment Group** rather than a wing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/125234/commanders-form-distinguished-list/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force Commanders form distinguished list &gt; Air Force &gt; Article Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air Force Commanders form distinguished list &gt; Air Force &gt; Article Display</span></span></span>

## When the 509th Bombardment Wing Was Created

The distinction becomes clear when the chronology is examined.

The Air Force lineage is:<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/434051/509-weapons-squadron-amc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dafhistory.af.mil">[dafhistory.af.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dafhistory.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

* **10 July 1946:** the wartime 509th Composite Group became the **509th Bombardment Group, Very Heavy**.
* **3 November 1947:** the Air Force established the **509th Bombardment Wing, Very Heavy** on paper.
* **17 November 1947:** the wing was formally organised at Roswell Army Air Field, and the existing bombardment group became one of its operational components.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/434118/509-bomb-wing-acc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force509 Bomb Wing (ACC) &gt; Air Force &gt; Fact Sheet Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 3, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 2015</span></span></span>

This sequence means that every event associated with the Roswell recovery during early July 1947 occurred before the wing existed. Even though Colonel Blanchard later commanded both the group and the newly organised wing, those were separate commands established at different times.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/125234/commanders-form-distinguished-list/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force Commanders form distinguished list &gt; Air Force &gt; Article Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air Force Commanders form distinguished list &gt; Air Force &gt; Article Display</span></span></span>

## Why the Naming Error Appears So Often

Several factors have encouraged the later title to replace the historically correct one.

First, the **509th Bombardment Wing** survived as the continuing strategic organisation after the group was eventually inactivated, inheriting much of the group's lineage, honours and public identity. Modern Air Force histories therefore focus heavily on the wing because it remains the active descendant organisation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/434118/509-bomb-wing-acc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force509 Bomb Wing (ACC) &gt; Air Force &gt; Fact Sheet Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 3, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 2015</span></span></span>

Second, many books, documentaries and media reports have relied on later Air Force terminology without checking the unit's organisational status in July 1947. Since both organisations carried the same numerical designation and shared many of the same personnel, substituting "Wing" for "Group" can seem harmless even though it is historically inaccurate.

Finally, retrospective storytelling often compresses organisational changes. Authors writing decades after the event sometimes refer to the better-known modern title, unintentionally projecting a later command structure back onto the events of 1947.

## Why the Distinction Matters in Roswell Accounts

Calling the responding organisation the **509th Bombardment Wing** does not, by itself, change the [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) surrounding the Roswell incident. However, it does matter for historical accuracy.

Using the correct organisational title:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* places documents and witness accounts within the proper military chain of command;
* avoids introducing an organisation that did not yet exist;
* helps distinguish contemporary records from later institutional histories; and
* demonstrates careful attention to chronology, an important consideration in a case where dates, documents and official actions remain closely scrutinised.

</div>

The error also illustrates a broader problem in Roswell literature: later terminology is sometimes applied retrospectively, making historical records appear more straightforward than they actually were. Recognising that the July 1947 responders belonged to the **509th Bombardment Group** rather than the later **509th Bombardment Wing** helps keep the documentary record aligned with the military organisation that actually operated at Roswell during the incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/434118/509-bomb-wing-acc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Air Force509 Bomb Wing (ACC) &gt; Air Force &gt; Fact Sheet Display</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 3, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 2015</span></span></span>

        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });

      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
