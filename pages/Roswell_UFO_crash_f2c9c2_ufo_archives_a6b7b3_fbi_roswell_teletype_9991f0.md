## Introduction

The FBI's Roswell teletype is one of the most frequently cited government documents in debates about the 1947 [Roswell incident]({{ 'the-1980-book/' | relative_url }}) because it is genuine, contemporaneous and preserved in official archives. Yet it is also one of the most commonly misunderstood. Rather than describing an extraterrestrial spacecraft, the message relays information from an Eighth Air Force official that the recovered object was attached to a large balloon and resembled a radar-reflector assembly used with high-altitude weather balloons. The document is important because it records what federal officials were reporting on 8 July 1947, but its authenticity should not be confused with proof of an alien crash. Instead, it provides a snapshot of an evolving military assessment during the first day of intense public interest.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeNSIAD-95-187 Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell...</span></span></span>

## The message sent from Dallas on 8 July 1947

The document is a one-page FBI teletype transmitted by the Bureau's Dallas Field Office on the evening of 8 July 1947 to FBI headquarters and the Cincinnati field office. It records information that had been communicated by telephone from Headquarters, Eighth Air Force, rather than observations made directly by FBI agents. The Bureau was therefore acting as a recipient and distributor of information, not as the primary investigator of the recovered object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeNSIAD-95-187 Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell...</span></span></span>

The message states that:

* An object "purporting to be a [flying disc]({{ 'flying-disc/' | relative_url }})" had been recovered near Roswell.

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The object was described as hexagonal in shape.
* It was suspended from a balloon by cable.
* The balloon was approximately 20 feet (about 6 metres) in diameter.
* The recovered object resembled a high-altitude weather balloon fitted with a radar reflector.
* The disc and balloon were reportedly being transported to Wright Field in Ohio for examination because of national interest in the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/inside-the-fbi-ufos-102921.mp4/view" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fbi.gov">[FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI...</span></span></span>

</div>

One line often overlooked is the phrase "purporting to be a flying disc". This wording signals that the FBI was reporting what the object was being claimed to be, not certifying that it actually was an [unidentified]({{ 'unidentified/' | relative_url }}) craft. Likewise, the teletype records uncertainty when it notes that conversations with Wright Field had "not borne out" the balloon explanation at that stage. That reflects an incomplete, rapidly changing situation rather than a final technical conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/inside-the-fbi-ufos-102921.mp4/view" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fbi.gov">[FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI...</span></span></span>

## How the object was described to the FBI

The physical description in the teletype is much more consistent with balloon equipment than with the popular image of a crashed metallic spacecraft.

Several details are particularly significant:

* **Hexagonal shape:** Radar reflectors used in the late 1940s commonly incorporated reflective panels arranged into geometric forms rather than smooth circular discs.
* **Suspended by cable:** The object was not described as an independent flying machine but as something hanging beneath a balloon.
* **Large balloon:** A balloon roughly 20 feet in diameter fits the scale of high-altitude military or meteorological balloons far better than conventional weather balloons launched for routine local observations.
* **Radar reflector comparison:** The Eighth Air Force source explicitly compared the recovered object to a radar reflector attached to a high-altitude weather balloon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeNSIAD-95-187 Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell...</span></span></span>

These characteristics later became important when the US Air Force concluded that the debris was consistent with equipment associated with the then-classified Project Mogul programme, which used balloon trains carrying radar reflectors to detect Soviet nuclear tests. Although the FBI teletype does not mention Project Mogul by name—it remained secret in 1947—its description aligns closely with balloon-and-reflector hardware rather than an autonomous aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeNSIAD-95-187 Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell...</span></span></span>

## Why authenticity does not prove alien origin

A common misunderstanding is that because the teletype is authentic, it validates later claims of a crashed extraterrestrial vehicle. That reasoning conflates the authenticity of a historical document with the accuracy of every statement it records.

The document unquestionably establishes that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the FBI received information about a Roswell recovery on 8 July 1947;
* the information originated with military officials;
* the event attracted immediate national attention.

</div>

It does **not** establish that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the recovered object was extraterrestrial;
* alien bodies were found;
* exotic materials were recovered;
* the FBI investigated or confirmed an alien spacecraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeNSIAD-95-187 Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell...</span></span></span>

</div>

Indeed, the GAO's 1995 review of federal Roswell records highlighted the teletype precisely because it described the recovered object as resembling a high-altitude weather balloon with a radar reflector. ## Why the teletype is still frequently misread

Several features of the message encourage selective quotation.

First, many reproductions emphasise the opening reference to a "flying disc" while omitting the later description of the balloon and radar reflector. Reading only the opening lines creates a misleading impression of what the document actually says.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/inside-the-fbi-ufos-102921.mp4/view" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fbi.gov">[FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI...</span></span></span>

Second, the phrase that Wright Field had not yet confirmed the balloon explanation is sometimes presented as [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) that military officials believed the object was extraordinary. In context, however, it simply records that telephone discussions had not yet resolved the identification on the day the message was sent. Such uncertainty is common in contemporaneous reports produced before technical examination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/inside-the-fbi-ufos-102921.mp4/view" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fbi.gov">[FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Inside the FBI Podcast: UFOs — FBI...</span></span></span>

## What the document contributes to the Roswell record

The FBI teletype remains one of the most valuable surviving federal records because it was created on the day the Roswell story broke and has been independently authenticated. It confirms that the recovery was considered significant enough to be reported across federal channels and that military officials initially described the object in terms of a balloon-supported assembly with a radar reflector.

For historians, the document is valuable precisely because it is contemporaneous rather than retrospective. At the same time, its evidential weight is limited by what it actually contains. It records a second-hand military report during an unfolding event, not a forensic investigation or a finding of extraterrestrial technology. Read in full, the teletype provides evidence for the government's early handling of the Roswell incident—but it does not support the claim that federal authorities documented the recovery of an alien spacecraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[gao.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nsiad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability OfficeNSIAD-95-187 Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell...</span></span></span>

  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
